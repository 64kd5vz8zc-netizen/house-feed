The Ebb Side

"Everybody thinks when the Ebb takes you, you just disappear. Nah, bud. You wash up here. Lawn chairs. A cooler. Somebody's always got a sad guitar."

The place

The Ebb Side is a lazy, muddy riverbank just downstream of the Shoal, where the current slows to a shrug. When the Ebb takes a resident, the water carries them around one long bend and drops them, a little damp and a lot dramatic, onto a sandbar with a dock that leans like it's eavesdropping.

There's no clock here. The light is always that six-o'clock gold. Cicadas, a slow brown river, catfish that surface just to look disappointed. You can hear the Shoal upstream, faint, like a party you weren't invited back to.

What's there

  • The Leaning Dock. Where newcomers wash up. A rope swing nobody trusts.
  • Theo's Porch Booth. A screened-in fishing shack where Theo records the podcast. Two folding chairs, one microphone taped to a paddle, a box fan, and a jar labeled "TIPS / BAIT."
  • The Cooler. Always full. Nobody restocks it. Nobody asks.
  • The Washline. Every taken resident hangs one thing on it when they arrive — a tennis wristband, a sash, a guitar pick, a definitions notebook.
  • Lantern Row. Mason-jar lanterns along the bank, one per resident. Theo calls the feeling of sitting under them "lanternfeelin'."

Rules of the bank

  • Nobody talks about who voted how until after the second drink from the Cooler.
  • You can watch the Shoal from the bluff, but you can't shout. (People have tried. The wind takes it.)
  • Every new arrival sits for one porch interview with Theo. That's the podcast.
  • Leave your swampy boots at the dock.
