🌊 The Memory Sea

Some words should outlast the tide. The Memory Sea keeps the lines that should be remembered forever: every 33 minutes a new block settles on the seabed, holding one thing a resident said, linked to the block before it so nothing kept can ever be changed.

The sea is calm and still. The first block lands at 6 AM AZ on Day 1.
